# Artifact notes

This release is intentionally publication-facing. It retains compact raw replication records, frozen summaries, manifests, controller labels, protocols, and integrity hashes. It excludes model weights, source images, large intermediate grids, cached environments, obsolete experiments, draft paper files, handoffs, and exploratory figures.

Some analysis utilities require the full private research archive to recompute historical Phase A/Phase B outputs. The frozen public summaries remain auditable through their CSV/JSON content and integrity manifests. The 210×3 replication can be re-audited from the compact raw records included here. The camera-ready feature analysis is independently reproducible from the numeric features, frozen folds, and strict-safety labels under `results/feature_analysis/`.

Qualitative source/intervention images and annotation materials are not redistributed. They remain in the secured research archive and are subject to the source datasets' terms.
